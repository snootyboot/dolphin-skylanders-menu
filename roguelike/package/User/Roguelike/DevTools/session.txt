# Session 1 - finds gold, health, XP and position in memory.
# Press End after each step. Page Down skips a step, Delete undoes the last one.
title: Session 1 - gold, health, XP and position
ready | Get into a level with one Skylander on the portal. Stand still at full health, then press End.
still | Don't move or touch anything for 5 seconds, then press End.
gold_1 | Pick up some gold. Wait until the gold counter stops, then press End.
gold_2 | Pick up more gold. Wait until the counter stops, then press End.
hit_1 | Let an enemy hit you once (lose some health, but don't get knocked out), then press End.
hit_2 | Let an enemy hit you once more, then press End.
heal | Eat food to get some health back, then press End.
xp_1 | Defeat one enemy and wait a few seconds, then press End.
xp_2 | Defeat another enemy and wait a few seconds, then press End.
spot_a | Walk to a spot you'll remember and stand still, then press End.
spot_b | Walk a good distance away and stand still, then press End.
spot_a_again | Walk back to the first spot as closely as you can, stand still, then press End.
still_2 | Don't move for 5 seconds, then press End.
swap | Open the quick menu and swap to a different Skylander. Once they appear, stand still and press End.
